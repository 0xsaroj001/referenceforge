## Provider technical eligibility

A callable service must answer real requests for a supported concrete Intent. Reviewers test correctness and quality independently, including new realistic inputs and failure cases. Show uptime, capacity, speed, cost, useful coverage and mission-app impact. Every claimed Miner–Intent pair needs its own relevant tests and same-Intent comparison. Claim ranking, requests or earnings only with matching network evidence.

## Provider judging

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Independently tested intelligence quality | 35 |
| Reliability, availability and capacity | 15 |
| Verified Intent coverage and application enablement | 10 |
| Verified use by H2 mission apps | 10 |
| Cost, latency and serving economics | 5 |
| Integration and documentation | 5 |
| Organic growth, education and developer activation | 20 |
| Total | 100 |

Winning requires at least **70/100** and **24/35 for intelligence quality**, plus eligibility. These are judge-assigned scores, not our test count or an Explorer ranking.

Consumption points apply once per Provider entrant across its qualifying pairs: up to 4 for qualifying app use and independent consuming groups, 2 for meaningful repeat use, 2 for ranked routing, and 2 for matched delivery/accounting evidence. Own-team app use can earn the first app point but not extra independent-group credit. Repeat-use thresholds include at least three meaningful executions with different inputs and qualifying use on two UTC dates. Direct calls do not earn ranked-routing points; verified direct use can still support use/repeat/accounting criteria. Empty traffic and additional Intent labels create no extra credit.

## Awards

Main competition: $7,000. Separate December challenges reserve $3,000 and have their own submissions, criteria and deadlines.

Providers receive four delivery awards of $300 and excellence awards of $500 and $300. A qualifying group can receive one delivery and one excellence award in the same track. Related wallets/teams do not create independent entrants. Passing a threshold guarantees no award.

Provider delivery selection first favors distinct enabled commercial missions, then distinct enabled/unblocked/improved H2 app workflows, then independently verified missing mission-critical Intent needs, then combined technical quality. Remaining Provider ties consider verified mission-app consumption and Provider quality. A new useful capability can qualify through repeatable mission-specific tests while downstream apps are still being built. Do not count the same mission, app group or result repeatedly.

Apps & Agents have $3,000, Evaluators $2,000. The user separately authorized a V2 Evaluator contribution on 29 October; no App-track entry is active. Old rank_answer/Q32.32 WASM is not a current evaluator submission.

The current Evaluators rules permit adapters, evaluation material, or both, including useful material before live activation. Weights are 40 measurement validity/discrimination/baseline, 15 integrity/robustness, 10 reproducibility/protocol compatibility, 10 useful coverage/improvement, 5 documentation/integration and 20 organic growth/education/developer activation. Eligibility plus 70/100 overall and at least 28/40 measurement validity are required. A fair comparable baseline is needed if one exists; credible new/underserved coverage requires reference quality and useful tests. Our [12-pair synthetic seed](../evaluator-v2/README.md) does not yet establish independent model discrimination or these judging thresholds.

For both tracks, the 20 growth points cover useful original public explanation/tutorials (5), reproducible onboarding and a working safe example with costs/troubleshooting (5), at least three meaningful updates on separate dates and substantive technical distribution/discussion (5), and independently verified developer activation/contribution attributable to the work (5). Views, likes, author-controlled traffic and posting alone do not establish these outcomes. Local preparation does not authorize posting or outreach.

## Submission and rights

Provide project/team/contact, named track, chosen concrete Intents and mission relevance, usable reviewer access, a 2–5 minute demo or accessible equivalent, beginner-friendly reproduction steps, costs/limits, test and network evidence, a useful public summary and explicit showcase/buyer-introduction permissions. Permission to share assets/contact details is separate from technical scoring.

Social views, likes and tags are not technical eligibility conditions or a change in judging weights. No fabricated receipts, collusive traffic, manufactured adoption or disguised related groups. Private data, keys and protected evaluation materials remain confidential. Cash-prize country, identity, tax and payout conditions require the organizer's published payment terms; our eligibility is not yet verified. A documented platform issue may lead to organizer-announced relief, not an assumed extension.
